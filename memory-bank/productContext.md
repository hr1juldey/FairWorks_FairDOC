# Product Context

This document describes the "why" behind the Fairdoc AI Triage System, the problems it solves, how it should work, and its user experience goals.

## Why this project exists:
- To address the growing demand for accessible and efficient healthcare guidance.
- To offload non-critical inquiries from medical professionals, allowing them to focus on urgent cases.
- To empower individuals with immediate, reliable health information.

## Problems it solves:
- Long wait times for medical advice.
- Misinformation from unreliable sources.
- Overburdened emergency services.
- Lack of immediate guidance for non-life-threatening symptoms.

## How it should work:
- Patients interact with an AI agent by describing their symptoms.
- The AI uses a combination of natural language processing, medical knowledge, and predefined protocols (like NICE guidelines) to assess the situation.
- Based on the assessment, the AI provides a clear recommendation: self-care, consult a GP, or seek emergency care.
- The system should be able to ask clarifying questions to gather more information.

## User experience goals:
- **Trustworthy:** Users must feel confident in the advice provided.
- **Easy to use:** The interface should be intuitive and accessible to all.
- **Fast:** Provide quick responses to urgent queries.
- **Empathetic:** The AI's tone should be supportive and understanding.
- **Private:** Ensure patient data is handled with utmost confidentiality and security.
