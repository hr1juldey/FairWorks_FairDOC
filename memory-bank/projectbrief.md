# Project Brief

This document outlines the core requirements and goals of the Fairdoc AI Triage System.

## Core Requirements:
- Develop an AI-powered medical triage system.
- The system should accurately assess patient symptoms and provide appropriate guidance (e.g., self-care, routine doctor consultation, emergency alert).
- The system should be robust and handle various medical scenarios.
- The system should be continuously learning and optimizable.

## Goals:
- Improve efficiency in medical pre-screening.
- Reduce unnecessary emergency room visits.
- Provide timely and accurate medical advice to patients.
- Ensure patient safety through reliable triage.
